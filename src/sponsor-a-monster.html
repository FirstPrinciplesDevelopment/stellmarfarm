Sponsor A Monster

2025

Stellmar Farm invites any local businesses to participate in our Sponsor A Monster event. There is no cost other than cost of materials. If you are interested, send a message to info@stellmarfarm.com stating your intention to participate. We will respond with the official acceptance.

Details:

  • Come up with an idea for a monster that will be installed in our corn maze from September 26th until Halloween.
  • Create the monster with dimensions: 6-7 feet tall. See links below for past monsters.
  • Complete the monster and delivery it to Stellmar Farm by September 20th.
  • Please include your business information in the display.
  • Also please add a solar light of some sort for the night time corn maze experiences.
